# Transseries

One-parameter [`Transseries`](@ref) ``\sum_n \sigma^n e^{-nA/\hbar}\Phi_n``, and
[`MultiTransseries`](@ref) for several actions at once, stored sparsely over the action
lattice.

The one- and many-action types share most of their interface, and the accessors below dispatch
on both.

## Resonance

When the actions satisfy integer relations the transseries develops logarithms.
[`resonance_lattice`](@ref) computes the relations exactly over ``\mathbb{Z}``, and
[`resonant_solve`](@ref) is the primitive that generates the log tower.


## Solving ODEs

[`transseries_solve`](@ref) is a model-agnostic driver that builds a transseries solution
sector by sector from a user-supplied linear solve. [`multi_transseries_solve`](@ref) is the
several-actions form, and it handles the *resonant* case: when the actions satisfy an integer
relation the sectors carry a tower in ``\log``, and the tower's blocks are solved from the top
down, because the obstruction in block ``p`` is what fixes the free constant of block ``p+1``.

## Painlevé I

[`painleve1`](@ref) applies the driver to Painlevé I, with both normalization conventions of
the instanton action available (`:gikm` by default, `:string` for the string-equation
convention).

[`painleve1_two_parameter`](@ref) builds the two-parameter transseries, whose action pair
``(A, -A)`` is resonant. The logarithms it develops are not spread evenly over the sectors:
the resonant diagonal ``n = m`` is log-free at every order, and the tower begins at ``(2,1)``
and ``(1,2)``, generated by the kernel of the *one-instanton* operator. The homogeneous
solution behind the growth is therefore the sector that defines the transseries parameter.
[`painleve1_stokes_constant`](@ref) measures the Stokes constant either from the large-order
growth of the perturbative sector or directly from a Borel–Padé lateral jump. The two routes
are independent, and their agreement pins the factor of 2 in the large-order formula.
