Topological strings

A topological-string free energy is the canonical divergent series of the subject, and two Calabi-Yau geometries have one in closed form. Both are available here as ordinary series, so the rest of the package applies to them.

Resurgence.jl is a consumer here. It never computes a Gromov-Witten or Gopakumar-Vafa invariant, never runs topological recursion, and never runs the holomorphic-anomaly recursion. Everything below is a quoted closed form. For a geometry without one, generate the $F_g$ elsewhere and feed them in as a FormalSeries on the same grid.

The worked example, with figures, is examples/topological_string_resurgence.ipynb.

Background

The topological string on a Calabi-Yau threefold $X$ has a genus expansion in the string coupling $\lambda$,

\[F(\lambda, t) = \sum_{g \ge 0} \lambda^{2g-2} F_g(t), \qquad F_g(t) = \sum_{\beta} N_{g,\beta}\, Q^{\beta}, \qquad Q = e^{-t},\]

where $N_{g,\beta}$ are the genus-$g$ Gromov-Witten invariants and $t$ is the complexified Kähler parameter. The sum over $g$ diverges factorially, and the Borel singularities of $F$ in $\lambda$ sit at $2\pi i$ times periods of $X$: the central charges of D-branes. Reading those actions and their multiplicities off the $F_g$ is what this package is for.

Grid convention

Both constructors return a series in $\lambda$ on the unit $\lambda$ grid: power offset 2, odd coefficients exactly zero, and the coefficient of $\lambda^{2g-2}$ equal to $F_g$. This is not cosmetic. It makes borel divide genus $g$ by $\Gamma(2g-2) = (2g-3)!$, which is precisely the normalization in which the gap series' Borel transform is elementary (conifold_gap_borel). Expressed in $x = \lambda^2$ instead, the same series is Gevrey-2 and nothing closes.

The price is that large_order_fit does not apply: it requires nonzero coefficients and Gevrey-1 growth. Extract the action from the genus subsequence with richardson instead,

\[\frac{F_g}{F_{g+1}}\,(2g-2)(2g-1) \;\longrightarrow\; A^2 = (2\pi i t)^2 .\]

Free energies

Resurgence.conifold_gap_series — Function
conifold_gap_series(n::Integer; t = 1//1, var = :λ) -> FormalSeries

The conifold-gap free energy as a formal series in the string coupling λ, with n genus terms (genus 2 through n+1):

\[F(λ, t) = \sum_{g≥2} \frac{B_{2g}}{2g(2g-2)}\, t^{2-2g}\, λ^{2g-2} .\]

This is the universal behaviour of the topological-string free energies of any Calabi-Yau near a conifold point, with t the flat coordinate vanishing there (Huang-Klemm-Quackenbush 2007, "the gap condition"); it is equally the c = 1 string at self-dual radius, and the leading t → 0 term of the resolved conifold (resolved_conifold_series).

The result lives on the unit λ grid with power offset 2 and exactly-zero odd coefficients, so that borel divides genus g by Γ(2g-2) = (2g-3)!. In that normalization the Borel transform is elementary,

\[\mathcal{B}[F](ζ) = \frac1t\left[\frac{-1}{4z\sinh^2(z/2)} + \frac1{z^3} - \frac1{12 z}\right], \qquad z = ζ/t ,\]

see conifold_gap_borel: a tower of double poles at $ζ = 2πi m t$, $m ≠ 0$. Coefficients are exact Rational{BigInt} for rational t.

julia> F = conifold_gap_series(3);

julia> coefficients(F)
5-element Vector{Rational{BigInt}}:
 -1//240
   0
  1//1008
   0
 -1//1440

Resurgence.resolved_conifold_series — Function
resolved_conifold_series(n::Integer; Q, var = :λ) -> FormalSeries

The all-genus Gromov-Witten free energy of the resolved conifold as a formal series in the string coupling λ, with n genus terms (genus 2 through n+1) and Kähler parameter Q = e^{-t}:

\[F(λ, t) = \sum_{g≥2} (-1)^{g-1}\,\frac{B_{2g}}{2g\,(2g-2)!}\, \mathrm{Li}_{3-2g}(Q)\; λ^{2g-2} .\]

These F_g are quoted, not computed: they are the genus expansion of the Gopakumar-Vafa resummation $\sum_{d≥1} Q^d / (d\,(2\sin(dλ/2))^2)$ (Gopakumar-Vafa

  1. of the single invariant $n^0_1 = 1$. For 3-2g ≤ -1 the polylogarithm is a

rational function of Q, so the coefficients are exact Rational{BigInt} whenever Q is rational.

Layout matches conifold_gap_series (unit λ grid, power offset 2, zero odd coefficients); as t → 0 the coefficients reduce to that gap series at t ↦ i t, since $\lim_{Q→1}(1-Q)^{n+1}\mathrm{Li}_{-n}(Q) = n!$. The Borel singularities sit on the lattice returned by resolved_conifold_actions, and the Stokes jumps across the corresponding rays are where Donaldson-Thomas invariants enter (Alim-Saha-Teschner-Tulli 2023, Alim 2024).

Borel-plane data

The gap series is the package's first example with double Borel poles, which has two consequences. pade renders each as a coalescing pair of simple poles straddling the true location, so the pair's midpoint is the singularity and the splitting is the diagnostic. stokes_constant's residue route does not apply, since it is exact for simple poles only; the exact Laurent data is available analytically from conifold_gap_stokes_data instead.

Resurgence.conifold_gap_borel — Function
conifold_gap_borel(ζ; t = 1) -> Number

The exact Borel transform of conifold_gap_series, in closed form:

\[\mathcal{B}[F](ζ) = \frac1t\left[\frac{-1}{4z\sinh^2(z/2)} + \frac1{z^3} - \frac1{12 z}\right], \qquad z = ζ/t .\]

The two subtracted terms remove the genus-0 and genus-1 poles at the origin, leaving a function analytic at ζ = 0 whose only singularities are double poles at $ζ = 2πi m t$, $m ≠ 0$ - the exact statement behind the gap condition's large-order behaviour. For |ζ/t| < 1 the Bernoulli series is used instead of the closed form, which there suffers catastrophic cancellation.

This is the oracle for the whole file: borel(conifold_gap_series(n; t)) must reproduce its Taylor coefficients exactly, and laplace_sum through a Padé approximant must reproduce its Laplace integral numerically.

conifold_gap_actions(; t = 1, mmax = 3) -> Vector{Complex}

The Borel singularities $ζ_m = 2πi m t$ of conifold_gap_series for $0 < |m| ≤ mmax$, sorted by modulus. For real t these sit on the imaginary axis, so the Stokes rays are $θ = ±π/2$.

Resurgence.conifold_gap_stokes_data — Function
conifold_gap_stokes_data(m::Integer; t = 1) -> NamedTuple{(:action, :double, :residue)}

The Laurent data of conifold_gap_borel at its m-th singularity $ζ_m = 2πi m t$:

\[\mathcal{B}[F](ζ) = \frac{c_2}{(ζ-ζ_m)^2} + \frac{c_1}{ζ-ζ_m} + O(1), \qquad c_2 = \frac{-t}{2πi m},\quad c_1 = \frac{-1}{4π^2m^2} .\]

The double pole is what distinguishes a topological-string free energy from the Euler / Airy oracles, and the $1/m^2$ in c_1 is the multicover weight of the single underlying state. With the package's disc = s₊ - s₋ convention (stokes_discontinuity) these give the exact discontinuity across the ray through $ζ_m$,

\[\mathrm{disc}_m = \left[\frac{2πi\,c_2}{λ} - 2πi\,c_1\right] e^{-ζ_m/λ} = \left[\frac{-t}{mλ} + \frac{i}{2πm^2}\right] e^{-2πi m t/λ} ,\]

summed over all m of the same sign on that ray.

Resurgence.resolved_conifold_actions — Function
resolved_conifold_actions(; t = 1, mmax = 2, nmax = 1) -> Vector{Complex}

The Borel singularities of resolved_conifold_series at Kähler parameter t = -log Q:

\[ζ_{m,n} = 2π m\,(t + 2πi n), \qquad m ≠ 0,\ n ∈ ℤ ,\]

for $0 < |m| ≤ mmax$ and $|n| ≤ nmax$, sorted by modulus. The n = 0 tower $ζ = 2π m t$ is the D2-brane tower whose $m$-fold covers carry the $1/m^2$ multicover weight; the $n ≠ 0$ shifts are the D0-brane charge. For real t the $n = 0$ singularities lie on the positive and negative real axes, so $θ = 0$ is a Stokes ray - this is the geometry in which the Stokes constants are read as Donaldson-Thomas invariants (Alim-Saha-Teschner-Tulli 2023).

The lattice is derived in this package (see the ledger at the top of src/topological_string.jl) and verified numerically against borel_pade_poles; it is not read off from the literature.
